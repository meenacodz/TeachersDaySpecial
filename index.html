It Started With a Classroom

We entered your classroom as ordinary students.

We didn't know your name properly.

We didn't know how much you would teach us.

And honestly… we didn't know
that some lessons would stay with us forever.

You Taught Us More Than Subjects

You taught us Books

but somewhere between those chapters,
you taught us something far more important.

You taught us to try again
when the answer was wrong.

To stand up when we failed.

To believe in ourselves
when we couldn't see anything special in us.

We Never Noticed…

We never noticed how many times
you repeated the same thing
just because we didn't understand it.

We never noticed the extra minutes
you spent explaining one more question.

We never noticed how often
you worried about our future
while we were busy worrying about tomorrow's homework.

Maybe that's what makes a teacher special.

You cared about a future
we hadn't even imagined yet.

One Day, We Will Leave

One day, these classrooms will become memories

These benches will have different students,
and the blackboard will carry different lessons.

We'll move on—
to different schools, cities, dreams, and lives.

But somewhere inside us,
a small part will always belong to this classroom.

And a part of this classroom
will always belong to you.

Some lessons are forgotten. Some teachers never are.

The Unseen Difference

Where Hearts Speak

We never noticed when it happened.
Somewhere between the greetings,
little conversations, and ordinary days,
you became more than just a teacher.

You noticed our smallest efforts,
encouraged us when we doubted ourselves,
and made us feel that we mattered.

Years may pass, and many things may fade,
but we'll remember how it felt
to be understood, cared for, and believed in.

Some people leave memories.
leave warmth behind.❤️


~ Some words were never written in our notebooks.

You Made Ancient Words Feel Alive

Some moments felt ordinary then—
a familiar voice, a simple correction,
a smile before the bell rang.

We didn't know those little moments
would someday become memories
we'd wish we could live once more.

The classroom may change,
the days may pass,
but something from those moments
will always remain within us.

Some bonds don't need words
become unforgettable.

~ Some lessons are older than us, yet stay with us forever.

You Taught Us That We Could Try Again

We got the answers wrong.
We erased them.
We tried again.

Sometimes, we thought we couldn't do it.
But you always said—
“Try again.”

You taught us that mistakes
aren't the end.

They are simply another step
towards finding the answer.

And perhaps, that's the lesson
we'll remember beyond Mathematics:

Keep trying.
Eventually, it makes sense.

~ Sometimes the answer wasn't the lesson. We were.

Oh... one last thing.

I made all of this for one reason...

To see you smile.

But... I haven't seen it yet.
